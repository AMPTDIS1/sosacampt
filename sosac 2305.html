Safety Tip of the Day

Stay proactive with VSA

🦺 PPE checks on the yard & at dispatch

Always be proactive with VSA on the yard. Check that all PPE is worn correctly — both on the yard and during dispatch. If you see something, say something.

✓ Do
Verify hi-vis, safety shoes & gloves before stepping on the yard.
✕ Don't
Walk past a missing-PPE situation — flag it, fix it.
Standard Tip of the Day

Finger Scan & end of shift

🔐 Use it, return it, finish strong

Use your finger scan while doing Pick & Stage and — very important — return it to the charging cabinet at the end of your shift.

Shift ends at 13:00, not before. We've seen AAs leaving the floor at 12:49 — that is not acceptable and counts as abandoning the job. If you have a genuine need, inform your supervisor or the manager on shift.
